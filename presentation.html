Section 02 • Problem & Proposed Solution

Eliminating Fragmentation in Student Club Operations

🚨 THE PROBLEM BEING ADDRESSED
  • Scattered Coordination: Essential action items get buried across WhatsApp groups, Google Docs, and chaotic spreadsheets.
  • Volunteer Burnout & Idleness: Task delegation is arbitrary—active volunteers get overloaded while others receive no tasks.
  • Meeting Note Decay: Critical decisions made during planning meetings are never converted into prioritized deliverables with deadlines.
  • Institutional Amnesia: Club knowledge, sponsorship contacts, and judging rubrics vanish when student leadership rotates annually.
💡 THE PROPOSED SOLUTION (CLUBOPS-AI)
  • Single Command Center: Integrates event planning, volunteer allocation, transcripts, risk mitigation, and documents into one system.
  • Dynamic Workload Balancing: Multi-action NLP parser segments conversational text, calculates 4-tier priorities, and auto-routes tasks to least-loaded volunteers.
  • Grounded RAG Repository: Indexes PDF, DOCX, DOC, and TXT with cosine similarity and verbatim citations—100% zero AI hallucination.
  • Autonomous AI Workflows: Natural language conversational agent performs live database actions across 6 dedicated application tools.

Section 04 • Approach & Implementation

Algorithmic Innovation & Key Technical Decisions

1. Multi-Sentence Splitting

Conversational transcripts frequently combine multiple directives. Our engine isolates distinct actions (e.g. separates stage lighting calibration from perimeter security) before creating discrete database tasks.

2. Least-Loaded Volunteer Allocation

Dynamic workload balancing algorithm analyzes active task counts across registered volunteers and routes newly identified tasks to specialists with the lowest active task counts.

3. 4-Tier Automated Priority Engine

Classifies urgency into Critical, High, Medium, and Low based on financial penalty clauses, equipment damage bonds, time constraints, and safety perimeter bottlenecks.

4. 2026 Relative Deadline Normalizer

Resolves relative statements ("by Friday 5 PM") into calendar-accurate 2026 ISO deadlines, eliminating century-old historic date bugs.
