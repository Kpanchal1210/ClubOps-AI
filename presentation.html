CLUBOPS • BIT N BUILD 2026 • PROBLEM & PROPOSED SOLUTION
The Status Quo vs. The ClubOps AI Approach
Comparing traditional fragmented coordination with intelligent autonomous operations.
THE PROBLEM LANDSCAPE
Operational Bottlenecks in Campus Clubs
  • • Fragmented Coordination: Tasks get buried across WhatsApp groups, Google Docs, and messy spreadsheets.
  • • Volunteer Overload & Burnout: Delegation is arbitrary; active volunteers get overloaded while others remain completely idle.
  • • Meeting Note Decay: Critical decisions made during meetings rarely translate into prioritized deliverables with deadlines.
  • • Institutional Amnesia: Club documents, sponsorship guidelines, and past event learnings vanish when student leadership rotates.
PROPOSED SOLUTION: CLUBOPS-AI
Unified Autonomous Operations Center
  • • Centralized Mission Control: Single hub for planning, tasks, volunteers, risks, documents, and notifications.
  • • Dynamic Auto-Delegation: NLP separates compound meeting statements and routes tasks to the least-loaded matching volunteer.
KEY IDEA BEHIND SOLUTION
Grounded Intelligence + Autonomous Workflows
  • • Grounded RAG: Answers queries strictly from club docs (.pdf, .docx, .doc) with verbatim citations (zero hallucination).
  • • Agentic Workflows: Natural language commands trigger live database actions via 6 specialized backend tools.

CLUBOPS • BIT N BUILD 2026 • APPROACH & IMPLEMENTATION
Algorithmic Precision & Technical Decisions
Key engineering innovations solving unstructured data, date decay, and workload fairness.
MULTI-SENTENCE STATEMENT SPLITTING
Compound Directives Segmentation

Conversational transcripts blend directives ('Aisha test stage lighting and David coordinate perimeter security'). Our engine uses regex and NLP delimiters to isolate compound clauses into discrete atomic actions before task creation.

DYNAMIC LEAST-LOADED VOLUNTEER ALLOCATION
Balanced Workload Routing

Prevents volunteer burnout by analyzing active task counts across registered volunteers in MongoDB ($lookup aggregate). The router identifies domain specialists (AV, Security, Logistics) and assigns tasks to the least-loaded member.

4-TIER AUTOMATED PRIORITY ENGINE
Urgency Lexical Analysis

Classifies tasks into CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW. Financial penalty bonds, equipment damage contracts, and stage power blockers trigger automatic escalation with countdown deadline tracking.

2026 RELATIVE DEADLINE NORMALIZER
Calendar-Accurate Timestamps

Replaced historic date bugs with parseTranscriptDeadline(), converting conversational strings ('by Friday 5 PM') into future ISO dates within the active year (2026), preventing century-old date distortion.
